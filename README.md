# Spotlight @crisxai5: AI & Crypto Expert on X

A make.fun bounty entry: a public X post introducing
[@crisxai5](https://x.com/crisxai5) as a must-follow AI and crypto expert,
covering AI agents, crypto insights, and Web3 innovation.

- **Bounty:** [Spotlight @crisxai5: AI & Crypto Expert on X](https://make.fun/c/u-tina-spotlight-crisxai5-ai-crypto-expert-on-x-7c1ed9ad)
- **Deadline:** 27 Nov 2026 18:35 WIB
- **Scoring:** likes + reposts + quotes on the X post, read after the deadline.

## The graphic

`crisxai5-spotlight.png` — an original spotlight card made in this session:
a dark, phone-readable design with a huge `@crisxai5`, the tagline
"Follow the signal, not the noise", and three pillars — AI Agents,
Crypto Insights, Web3 Innovation — under a MUST-FOLLOW stamp. The hook is
signal vs noise: sharp, scroll-stopping, and readable at a glance on a phone.

## The post

The post text is in [`post.txt`](post.txt). It carries `@crisxai5`,
https://x.com/crisxai5, the hashtags #AI #Crypto #AIAgents #Web3, and the
make.fun play code `makefun-barnyard-cabin-sunrise`, with the graphic
attached.

> ⚠️ **Status: the X post is NOT published yet.** `POST.json` still holds
> the placeholder URL and must be updated with the real post URL
> (`https://x.com/<handle>/status/<id>`) after the post goes live from a
> public X account, before the deadline.

## How the entry works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` from the
repository root to `/output/POST.json`, where the make.fun gate reads it.
The post itself is the entry: the house reads its public engagement
through FxTwitter after the deadline.

Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`out/POST.json` is then byte for byte this directory's `POST.json`.
